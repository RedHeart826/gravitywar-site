Bend your shot around the planets.

Gravity War is a turn-based space artillery duel for iPhone and iPad. Two ships, a few planets, and one shot each turn. Set your angle and power, watch gravity bend the path, and read your misses to land the hit.

One hit wins the round. Every shot leaves a ghost, and the reply-shot rule makes sure going first is never an advantage.

Every map is fair. Each world is checked to be solvable from both sides with comparable difficulty.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. It works fully offline.
