Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · Applies to the Gravity War app for iPhone and iPad

In short: Gravity War does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Your games and settings stay on your device.

Who we are

Gravity War is made by RedHeart. You can reach us at support@techsupport24.ca.

What stays on your device

The app saves your settings, your campaign progress, your statistics, and any match in progress, so you can pick up where you left off. This information is stored only on your device. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Downloading new levels

Gravity War can download new campaign levels without an app update. To do this it requests a list of available levels, and any new level files, from our website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. The request does not include any information about you, your device, your progress, or your settings.

As with any website, the hosting provider (GitHub) receives your device's IP address and standard technical information, such as the type of request, when the files are downloaded. We do not receive or store this information. GitHub's handling of it is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

You can turn this off at any time in the app's Settings with Download new levels. The game works fully offline.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not require an account or a sign-in.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. This is controlled by you in iOS Settings and is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Gravity War collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, for example when a new feature is added, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@techsupport24.ca.
