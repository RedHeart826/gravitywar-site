How to play

How do I aim?
Drag from your ship. The direction sets the angle and the distance sets the power. Use the arrows for fine adjustments: single arrows change by 0.1, double arrows by 1, and holding repeats. Press FIRE to shoot.
How do I win?
One hit destroys a ship. After the first player fires, the second always gets a reply shot, so if both ships are hit, the round is a draw and is replayed. Win the most rounds to win the match.
What are the faded lines?
Ghosts of your last shots, labeled with their angle and power. Use them to correct your aim. Compare mode (the curved-path button, or a long press on the field) highlights your last two shots and shows how close each came.
Can I zoom?
Pinch to zoom and drag with two fingers to look around. The target button recenters the view.

Common questions

Does the game need an internet connection?
No. Everything works offline. New campaign levels download in the background when you are online, and you can turn that off in Settings.
I left in the middle of a match. Is it lost?
No. Your match is saved after every shot. Choose Resume match on the main menu.
Is there a way to make the game calmer?
With Reduce Motion on (iOS Settings → Accessibility → Motion), Gravity War turns off slow motion, camera sweeps, and animated effects.
